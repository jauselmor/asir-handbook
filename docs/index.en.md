# Network Planning and Administration

Module 0370 of the *Administración de Sistemas Informáticos en Red* higher
vocational programme. 200 hours, first year.

## How this site works

Everything here is written in English first, and translated into Spanish. That
is on purpose. English is the working language of this industry, and getting
comfortable reading and writing it is part of the module, not an obstacle to
it. Use the language switcher when you need it — but try the English page first.

## Common floor, open ceiling

Every unit guarantees the learning outcomes of the qualification for everyone.
That is the floor, and it is not optional.

On top of that, most units carry a **Going further** section: optional depth
for anyone who wants to push past the syllabus. Nothing there is examinable.
Everything there is what the job actually looks like.

## Labs are tickets, not exercises

Wherever possible, practical work is framed as a real incident: something is
broken or missing, and you have to work out what and fix it. There is usually
more than one acceptable answer, and you will be asked to defend the one you
chose.
